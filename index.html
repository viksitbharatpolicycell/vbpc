About

What is VBPC?

The Viksit Bharat Policy Cell is a student-led, non-partisan policy think tank founded in 2025 and registered on the Government of India's MY Bharat Portal.

VBPC was built on a simple belief: student engagement with governance should not stop at reading about policy. It should produce documented, submitted, publicly archived policy outputs that hold up to institutional scrutiny.

Our work is governed by a formal Constitution and Bylaws. Every session follows a structured framework. Every brief goes through internal review before submission. Every submission is logged in this public archive with a permanent reference number.

VBPC is not affiliated with any political party and does not represent any government body. We are an independent student institution operating in the public interest.
